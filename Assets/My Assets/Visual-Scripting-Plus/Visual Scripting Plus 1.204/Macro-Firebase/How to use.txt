Using : 
. To turn on Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics on Android/iOS... to use Google Ads to get more Installs can be counted for your new Published Games

How to use :
. Step 1 : Install Firebase to your Project  : https://firebase.google.com/docs/unity/setup
. Step 2 : In Unity : Unzip Macro-FirebaseAnalytics & Add prefab Macro-Firebase to Hierarchy in your first Scene only. Done ! 

* Build Failed ?! : open Macro-Admob to read Note "How to use". They are same how to build !
* Work for Android & iOS build from Windows or Mac (ignore warning "the iOS build not support in Windows, it will work because final Build is on Xcode.)
